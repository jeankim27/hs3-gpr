# Reading list

Start here, in this order. Keys match [`refs/references.bib`](../refs/references.bib) and the
`source` fields in the register. Add new sources to the team's Zotero group library first.

| # | Key | What it is | Read it for | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `kaku2017` | Kaguya radar evidence for an intact lava tube at Marius Hills | Our science goal, the echo signature of a tube | everyone |
| 2 | `ono2010` | Kaguya Lunar Radar Sounder instrument paper | A flown lunar sounder: frequencies, antenna, timing | radar-hw, systems |
| 3 | `ono2009` | Kaguya results: subsurface layers under the nearside maria | What depths a few-MHz sounder actually reached | scene |
| 4 | `porcello1974` | Apollo 17 lunar sounder design | Design rationale and trade-offs for lunar sounding | radar-hw, systems |
| 5 | `seu2007` | SHARAD on Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter | Same architecture as ours: presum onboard, SAR on ground | processing, radar-hw |
| 6 | `kobayashi2012` | SAR processing of Kaguya sounder data | How SAR is applied to lunar sounder data | processing |
| 7 | `olhoeft1975` | Dielectric properties of lunar material | Permittivity values (`eps_*`) | scene |
| 8 | `heiken1991` | Lunar Sourcebook (electrical properties chapter) | Loss tangent vs composition (`tan_delta_*`), regolith | scene |
| 9 | `cumming2005` | SAR processing textbook | Pulse compression, back-projection, resolution | processing |
| 10 | `itur_p372` | ITU-R radio noise recommendation | Galactic noise vs frequency (EQ-07) | radar-hw |
| 11 | `warren2016` | gprMax open-source GPR simulator | Optional Q3 full-wave check of the tube response | scene |

**Rule of thumb:** when you use a number from a source, put the key in that variable's `source`
field and note the page or table in `notes`.
